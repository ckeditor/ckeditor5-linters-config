---
type: Major breaking change

scope:
  - eslint-config-ckeditor5
  - eslint-plugin-ckeditor5-rules
---

Upgraded ESLint to v10. The shared configuration and rules plugin now require `eslint@^10.0.0` and no longer support ESLint 9. Consequently, they require Node.js `^20.19.0 || ^22.13.0 || >=24` (as mandated by ESLint 10) and only support the flat configuration format.
